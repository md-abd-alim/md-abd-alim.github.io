Highland Park Dental

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Lakeland, FL

Business Overview

Highland Park Dental is a family practice at 4326 Highland Park Blvd in South Lakeland, Florida, led by Dr. Mical Slater, a 2007 Nova Southeastern University graduate with implant and cosmetic continuing education. It holds a 5.0 rating across 189 Google reviews, yet its search footprint is far smaller than its reputation.

This independent portfolio study is a 48-page Google Business Profile and local SEO growth strategy for a publicly visible practice, not a client engagement. It audits the profile, website, directories and Lakeland competitors, then plans category and service entries, Maps signals, service and area pages, reviews, citations, links, AI search, conversion fixes and compliance wording. It closes with a KPI framework and a 90-day roadmap with owners. Every recommendation is framed as a planning objective, to be measured against baselines captured in the first month.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Current Situation

The audit shows a trusted practice whose website, profile data and link profile do not yet match the quality of its patient reviews.

Strong brand, thin non-brand reach

Brand terms and a few Lakeland veneer searches rank well, including position 2 for lakeland veneers. But implant dentistry in Lakeland sits at position 22, dental implants highland park at 12, and teeth whitening near Lakeland at 58.

Traffic is informational

About 228 monthly visits are down 27%, and roughly two thirds of estimated traffic is informational. Transactional searches bring 36 visits from only 11 keywords, which is where revenue sits and where growth is most valuable.

Conflicting business data

Google shows 863-400-0130 and a suite number that the website lacks, while the site lists two other phone numbers. WebMD shows Friday hours and not accepting patients, and MapQuest still lists a legacy provider.

Thin revenue pages

The implant page runs roughly 200 words and the full-mouth restoration page about 100, with most sub-pages last edited in 2017. There are no area pages, no cost or financing page, and no FAQ blocks on service pages.

Weak authority signals

An Authority Score of 9 sits against about 1.1K referring domains and 1.3K backlinks, with anchors that suggest link-building patterns. AI visibility is zero, and a malformed crown-lengthening link appears in the cosmetic hub.

Wording that needs review

Titles such as Best Lakeland Dentist, a number-one cosmetic heading, a lifetime implant claim and a profile reference to periodontists, though none is identified on the site, may conflict with Florida advertising rules.

What Happens If These Issues Are Not Fixed

Left alone, the gap between reputation and search footprint is likely to persist or widen.

Continued traffic loss

With non-brand visibility thin and the existing trend already down 27%, the practice stays dependent on brand and informational searches that rarely become implant or veneer consultations.

Maps prominence stays capped

Mixed phone numbers, an unverified suite and provider listings that conflict make it harder for Google to trust one consistent entity, limiting what 189 strong reviews can do.

Rivals take high-value searches

Lakeland competitors publish deeper service and location pages. Short implant and full-mouth pages leave the most valuable cases to practices with more complete content.

Wasted visits and calls

If directories show wrong hours or not accepting patients, and calls go to unclear numbers, prospective patients may be lost before they ever reach the appointment page.

Compliance exposure

Superlatives, a lifetime-implant claim and a periodontist reference could draw regulatory scrutiny. Leaving them live also undermines the credibility the practice has built.

Absent from AI answers

With zero AI visibility, thin pages and inconsistent entity data, the practice is unlikely to be cited when patients ask assistants about implant dentists in Lakeland.

Recommendations & Action Plan

I recommend fixing data and compliance first, then rebuilding revenue pages, then building authority, so each step supports the next.

1. Reconcile business data

Choose one canonical phone number and confirm whether suite 1647 exists. Then align Google, the website, WebMD, MapQuest, Yelp and Facebook, remove the legacy provider listing, and confirm new-patient status before publishing it anywhere.

2. Optimize categories and services

Keep Dentist as primary, add Cosmetic dentist and Dental implants provider if missing, and treat emergency service as conditional on Monday to Thursday hours. Do not use periodontist or endodontist categories. Build service entries for implants, veneers and full-mouth restoration.

3. Rewrite the description and photos

Replace the profile text with the factual 467-character version in the report, free of superlatives. Grow the gallery from about 6 to 8 photos to 30 or more over 90 days, with consent for patient images.

4. Rebuild priority service pages

Expand implants, implant-retained dentures, veneers and full-mouth restoration with specifications, FAQs, cost and financing detail and consultation prompts, keeping existing URLs to protect rankings. Add a tracked appointment page and link the profile to it.

5. Add tiered area pages

Build dedicated pages for South Lakeland and Lakeland Highlands, then Highland City, Bartow and Winter Haven after verifying drive times and patient ZIP data. Smaller neighborhoods sit inside an Areas We Serve hub to avoid thin pages.

6. Run the review and citation system

Request reviews after every completed visit without incentives or sentiment filtering, and reply within two business days. Encourage natural detail about services and neighborhoods, and reconcile the top 15 directories.

7. Build local authority and links

Audit the existing backlink profile, disavowing only clear patterns, then earn links from the Lakeland chamber, schools, charities and health partners the practice already belongs to.

8. Add schema, AI content and compliance fixes

Add Dentist, Person and FAQ schema, put short answers at the top of service pages, and fix the broken link. Replace risky title and heading wording after the practice and counsel approve final text.

Roadmap

Month 1: Foundation

Rewrite the profile description, fix directory data, correct the broken link, titles and meta descriptions, run the compliance review, capture GBP, call, geo-grid and competitor baselines, and set up tracking.

Month 2: Pages and reviews

Rebuild the implant, denture, veneer and full-mouth pages, publish the first area pages and the financing page, start the review-request workflow, and release two implant or cosmetic guides.

Month 3: Authority and AI

Add schema, complete citations, begin local link outreach and the backlink audit, test AI search visibility, then report Day 90 results and set the next quarter's priorities.

Expected Outcomes

The report sets these as planning objectives to measure against a Month 1 baseline, not promises.

Traffic recovery

Return organic visits to about 310 a month by Day 90, the level implied before the 27% loss, then aim for 50% above the current baseline by Day 180.

Review growth

Reach 210 or more reviews by Day 90 and 235 or more by Day 180 while holding a 4.9 rating or higher.

Profile engagement

Improve calls, directions and website clicks by 15% on baseline at Day 90 and 30% at Day 180, once baselines are captured.

Local rankings

Improve average grid rank in core cells at Day 90 and reach the top five in 33813 cells by Day 180.

Authority and AI

Earn 10 or more quality local links by Day 90 and 20 or more by Day 180, with AI-cited pages rising from 8 to 12 and first mentions.

More From the Report

Competitor validation

Many organic competitor domains share the Highland Park name but serve other states, so the report separates them from true Lakeland rivals such as Walding and Associates, Lakeland Dentistry on the same street, and national brands. Review figures are third-party snapshots to be refreshed live in Month 1.

E-E-A-T and trust

Dr. Slater's Nova Southeastern degree and implant and cosmetic training become visible proof through an expanded doctor profile, license details, memberships and reviewed-by notes. Review excerpts are shown as real text on the site, and a clear financing and insurance page supports trust.

Conversion and technical fixes

Two phone paths for current and new patients are labeled, with click-to-call tested. The profile appointment link moves from the homepage to a tracked request page. Technical items include a robots.txt check, a footer log-in link to remove, and schema validation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Highland Park Dental get so little traffic despite 189 five-star reviews?

The audit finds that most of the roughly 228 monthly visits come from brand and informational searches. Revenue pages for implants and full-mouth restoration are short, and conflicting business data limits how much Google can reward the strong reviews.

Which services does the report prioritize?

Tier 1 is dental implants, implant-retained dentures, porcelain veneers and full-mouth restoration, because of case value and the practice's in-house capability. Restorative and cosmetic services follow, and emergency care stays an honest page, not a major investment.

What business data conflicts does the report flag?

Google lists 863-400-0130 and suite 1647, while the website shows two other numbers and no suite. WebMD shows Friday hours and not accepting patients, and MapQuest keeps a retired provider. I recommend one canonical set across every source.

Why does the report raise advertising wording?

Current titles and headings use phrases such as Best Lakeland Dentist and number one, and the profile mentions periodontists. The report cites Florida rules on laudatory claims and specialty advertising and suggests neutral wording, subject to review by the practice and counsel.

Does the report guarantee rankings or patients?

No. Day 90 and Day 180 figures are planning objectives measured against a Month 1 baseline. AI citations and local rankings cannot be guaranteed, and this is an independent portfolio study, not a client engagement.
