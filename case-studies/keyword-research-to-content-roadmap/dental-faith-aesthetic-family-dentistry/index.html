Dental Faith Aesthetic & Family Dentistry

Keyword Research to Content Roadmap · Nashville, TN

Business Overview

Dental Faith Aesthetic & Family Dentistry is a general, cosmetic and family practice at 390 Harding Pl #101 in Nashville, Tennessee (ZIP 37211), serving English- and Spanish-speaking patients. Its existing search visibility, as recorded in the workbook, leans heavily informational and branded, while commercial and transactional searches that bring in patients are barely captured.

This independent portfolio study is a 12-sheet keyword research and strategy workbook built to close that gap. It organizes 286 keywords into 75 clusters, reviews seven competitors, maps 78 pages (51 of them new), plans 189 internal links across service silos, and sets a six-month roadmap of 50 content items with 15 quick wins and a weighted scoring model. Search figures are labeled estimates, so the workbook is a planning tool rather than a traffic forecast.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

  • Roadmap and quick wins: 50 items over 26 weeks, 15 quick wins

Current Situation

The Business Analysis sheet shows a broad service menu and a bilingual practice, but a search profile that does not yet match the way patients buy.

Visibility skews informational

The workbook records the current intent mix as 69.0% informational, 9.1% navigational, 19.2% commercial and only 2.6% transactional. Transactional keywords produced about 14 traffic, so demand from patients ready to book is barely captured.

Planned mix reverses the balance

The keyword set is deliberately weighted toward action: 177 transactional keywords (61.9%), 37 commercial, 64 informational and 8 navigational. Funnel stages split into 185 bottom, 37 middle and 64 top of funnel.

A broad service menu

Services cover preventive care, cleanings, periodontal therapy, oral cancer screening, cosmetic work, crowns, bridges, veneers and whitening. The menu maps cleanly to a hub-and-spoke site structure, and the practice also has technology differentiators such as digital x-rays and impressions.

Local and bilingual gaps

Missing opportunities include neighborhood and ZIP terms (37211, Nolensville Pike, Antioch), same-day and open-Saturday searches, insurance and financing terms, family queries and Spanish-language local searches.

Content and conversion gaps

The analysis lists missing problem-based emergency content, cost guidance, per-service FAQs, technology explainers, comparison articles and proof pages. It also calls for Dentist and FAQ schema, click-to-call buttons, consistent NAP and clinician credentials.

A mixed competitor set

Competitors include Spanish-language directories, Siroco Dental, Buen Pastor Dental and local practices such as Nashville Family Dentistry and East Side Smiles. Most observations are marked estimated and note that few offer neighborhood-level Spanish content.

What Happens If These Issues Are Not Fixed

If the search profile stays informational and generic, the practice may keep attracting readers rather than patients ready to call.

Research traffic without bookings

With only 2.6% of the current profile transactional, visits could continue to come from learning queries while high-intent searches for implants, emergency care and cosmetic work go to competitors.

Spanish-speaking patients unreached

The practice serves Spanish-speaking patients, yet Nashville-specific Spanish pages are thin across the market. Without them, local Spanish searches may be answered by directories or other practices.

Neighborhood searches lost

Hyper-local terms such as dentist 37211 are not addressed. Nearby practices with strong review bases could capture map-pack and neighborhood demand that the practice sits closest to.

Emergency demand missed

Emergency and same-day searches carry strong intent and some of the highest cost-per-click estimates. Without a clear emergency hub and problem-based content, urgent patients may call another office.

Cost and insurance questions unanswered

Without cost-guidance, insurance and financing pages, patients comparing options have no answer on the site and may choose a practice that explains payment clearly.

Priorities driven by guesses

Search figures are estimates, and the homepage could not be crawled. Acting without crawl checks and live validation could misdirect effort or duplicate existing pages.

Recommendations & Action Plan

I use the workbook sheet by sheet: analyze, research, group, map, structure, schedule, then rank.

1. Business Analysis: set the baseline

This sheet defines goals, current intent mix, strengths, content gaps and page opportunities. It supports the first decision: shift focus from informational reach toward transactional and commercial terms, and confirm titles, H1s and schema with a crawl.

2. Keyword Master Database: choose targets

All keywords carry type, cluster, intent, funnel stage, estimated volume, difficulty, CPC, six scores, priority level, target URL and recommended action. Filtering Critical and High items shows the 166 terms to build around first.

3. Keyword Clusters: group by need

The 75 clusters pair each money topic, such as implants, emergency and veneers, with an educational cluster, giving each a primary keyword, questions, entities and a recommended page type.

4. Competitor Analysis: find the openings

Seven competitors are reviewed on ranking topics, weaknesses, content gaps and opportunities. The sheet supports choices such as owning Nashville Spanish content, adding emergency and technology depth, and building South Nashville and Antioch pages.

5. Keyword Mapping: assign every page

Each of 78 URLs gets a primary keyword, intent, existing or new status, action and cannibalization risk. Of these, 51 are new pages to create and 27 are existing pages to optimize.

6. Authority map, silos and linking

The Topical Authority Map and silo sheets organize cosmetic, restorative, preventive, periodontal and emergency topics into hubs with children. The 189-link blueprint links hubs to children, children back to hubs and every page toward the contact call to action.

7. Content Roadmap and Quick Wins: sequence

The 50 roadmap items run across six months, opening with homepage, implants, emergency and cosmetic hub optimization. Quick Wins lists 15 lower-difficulty targets, including Spanish terms such as dentista hispano nashville and dentist 37211.

8. Scoring Model: rank and re-rank

Six weighted factors give each keyword a 0 to 100 score with Critical, High, Medium, Low and Optional bands, so priorities can be recalculated as real search data replaces the estimates.

Roadmap

Quick wins

Optimize the homepage, implants, emergency and cosmetic pages, publish Spanish landing pages for the lower-difficulty terms, add a new-patient page, and create a South Nashville page for 37211.

Next

Build the remaining service hubs and children, add family, insurance and financing pages, strengthen internal links between hubs and children, and begin the first educational guides in months two and three.

Later

Expand comparison and cost guides, additional Spanish-language content and extra neighborhood pages in months four to six, then re-score priorities with live ranking and search data.

Expected Outcomes

The workbook states these goals as planning objectives rather than forecasts.

More local leads

Goal 1: generate more calls, bookings and direction requests through service, emergency, neighborhood and new-patient pages.

More organic traffic

Goal 2: increase organic traffic by covering the keyword clusters and educational topics currently missing.

Better commercial rankings

Goal 3: improve rankings for service-page, commercial and transactional keywords, shifting the intent mix toward the planned 61.9% transactional share.

Topical authority in every silo

Goal 4: build authority across each service silo through hub pages, supporting guides and 189 planned links.

Spanish-language local presence

The Spanish quick wins aim to reach Spanish-speaking Nashville patients with native-speaker reviewed pages.

More From the Report

Scoring model

Business value carries 25%, ranking opportunity 20%, and search demand, intent strength and conversion potential 15% each, with topical importance at 10%. The result is scaled to 100. Critical (90 and above) means build first, High (75 to 89) covers months 1 to 3, and Low terms are education content for when capacity allows.

Spanish-language opportunity

Twenty-two keywords are tagged Local - Spanish. Quick wins pair terms such as dentista hispano nashville, dentista de emergencia nashville and implantes dentales nashville with Spanish pages, FAQs, GBP service links and native-speaker review, since Spanish results are described as thinly optimized.

On-page and AI-ready recommendations

Beyond keywords, the analysis recommends LocalBusiness or Dentist plus FAQ schema, click-to-call buttons, NAP consistency, service-specific title and H1 patterns, before-and-after galleries, clinician credentials and Q&A blocks written so AI search tools can quote them.

What I Would Measure

  • Rankings for mapped priority keywords
  • Share of transactional keyword visibility
  • Organic traffic to service pages
  • Calls, bookings and direction requests
  • Spanish-language page visibility
  • Content roadmap completion

Important Notes

Volume, difficulty and CPC are estimates, and competitor observations are marked preliminary or estimated. The live homepage could not be rendered by the crawler, so on-page findings are limited, and a full crawl and live keyword data are needed before implementation. Priority scores do not predict rankings.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the central finding of this workbook?

Visibility skews informational: the recorded profile is 69.0% informational and only 2.6% transactional. The plan rebalances toward 177 transactional keywords tied to service, emergency, neighborhood and Spanish-language pages, so searchers ready to book can find the practice.

Why is Spanish-language content emphasized?

The practice serves English- and Spanish-speaking patients, and the workbook finds thin Nashville-specific Spanish content among competitors. Twenty-two keywords are tagged Spanish, and several quick wins call for native-speaker reviewed pages.

How many new pages does the plan propose?

The mapping sheet covers 78 pages: 51 new pages to create and 27 existing pages to optimize. The 189-link blueprint and 50-item roadmap then connect and schedule them over 26 weeks.

Are the search volumes real traffic numbers?

No. The roughly 149,200 combined monthly searches describe research scope, and volume, difficulty and CPC are labeled estimates. I recommend validating them with live tools and a site crawl before committing to a final page list.

What is the first thing to implement?

Month 1 starts with the homepage, implants, emergency and cosmetic hub optimization. Alongside that, the quick wins for Spanish terms and the 37211 hyperlocal page offer low-difficulty entry points with clear target pages.
