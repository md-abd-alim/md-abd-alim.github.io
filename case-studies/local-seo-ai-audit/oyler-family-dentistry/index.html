Oyler Family Dentistry

Local SEO & AI Audit · Greendale, IN

Business Overview

Oyler Family Dentistry is a family dental practice at 1500 Flossie Dr, Suite A, in Greendale, Indiana, with a 5.0 rating from 613 Google reviews, long-form service content and dentists with named credentials and memberships. Its reputation is strong, but several fixable gaps hold back how that strength shows up online.

This independent portfolio study is a 7-page digital presence and SEO audit dated August 26, 2026, based on public information rather than a client engagement. It scores the website, Google Business Profile, local SEO, social media, AI search readiness and conversion path, marks items that need owner access as Unable to Verify, lists the top 10 issues and ends with a prioritized 30-day plan. Every finding comes from public pages and live Google data, and the study separates what was verified from what needs owner access.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Current Situation

The audit finds a reputation and content base that are well ahead of the practice's local consistency and booking path.

Strong content and credibility

Content quality scores 9 of 10 and E-E-A-T 9 of 10. Service pages run long with FAQs and process detail, and the team page lists DDS and FAGD credentials, dental schools and ADA, Indiana Dental Association and Spear Education memberships.

City name split

The website, Google and Yelp use Greendale, while Facebook, Instagram, X, Nextdoor and a PatientConnect365 listing use Lawrenceburg for the same address. A blog title and a service-page title tag also say Lawrenceburg. NAP consistency scores 4 of 10.

Broken links and blank snippets

The Dental Implants page links to a contact.html URL that does not match the real /connect-with-us page and to a malformed relative link, giving internal linking 4 of 10. Its meta description is blank, and the Savings Program description is generic.

Weak local and image signals

Local landing pages score 4 of 10 because Aurora, Lawrenceburg and West Harrison have no dedicated pages. Several images carry empty alt text, blog posts show no publish dates, and speed and Core Web Vitals could not be tested.

Booking and proof gaps

Click-to-call scores 10 of 10, but the appointment button opens a request form, not live scheduling (5 of 10). No live chat was found (2 of 10), and no before-and-after gallery exists (2 of 10) despite heavy smile-transformation messaging.

Social and AI readiness

Facebook (8) and Instagram (7) are active, but no YouTube channel was found because videos sit on Vimeo. AI readiness scores 6: FAQ-style content helps, while the city split and blank metas muddy entity signals. No AI mentions could be verified.

What Happens If These Issues Are Not Fixed

If these gaps remain, the practice may not get full value from reviews and content it has already earned.

Diluted local identity

Two city names for one address spread citations and social profiles across different signals, which can make it harder for search engines and AI tools to resolve a single, consistent practice entity.

Missed clicks on key pages

A blank meta description on the implant page, one of the highest-value pages, leaves the search snippet to chance and may weaken click-through from search results.

Broken links hurt trust and crawling

Unreliable links on the implant page can send visitors and crawlers to dead ends, undermining a page that otherwise scores very well for depth.

Cosmetic message lacks proof

The site stresses smile transformations but shows no before-and-after gallery, so prospective patients cannot see the evidence for the service the practice markets hardest.

Friction in the patient journey

A request form with a callback, and no live chat, may lose visitors who want instant answers or a confirmed slot before they pick up the phone.

Unused video and freshness signals

Videos left only on Vimeo earn no YouTube discovery, and undated articles make freshness impossible for readers and crawlers to judge.

Recommendations & Action Plan

I recommend starting with the fixes that need no new content, then adding conversion proof and monitoring, in the order of the report's 30-day plan.

1. Standardize the city name

Choose one city, with Greendale matching the official Google and Yelp listing, and apply it across the website, Facebook, Instagram, X, Nextdoor and the PatientConnect365 listing. Update the blog title and URL and the implant page title tag to match.

2. Repair the implant page links

Point the contact.html link to /connect-with-us and correct the relative link for implant failure and salvage so it resolves to the right path. Then recrawl the page to confirm that no broken links remain.

3. Write the missing meta descriptions

Draft a benefit-driven description for the Dental Implants page, which is currently blank, and replace the generic Savings Program text. Review other core pages for thin descriptions using the same pattern.

4. Build a before-and-after gallery

Create a simple gallery page and link it from the Advanced Smile Care and People Love It Here pages. Use only cases with patient consent, since the practice already leads with cosmetic transformation content.

5. Launch a YouTube channel

Re-upload the existing hero, team and technology videos from Vimeo, with descriptions that link back to oylerdds.com. This adds search and discovery value from content the practice has already produced.

6. Add dates and alt text

Show publish or updated dates on the 17 or more live articles through a template change, and add short descriptive alt text to the homepage and service-page images found without it.

7. Validate with owner data

Use Search Console to confirm the fixes, and have the owner review Google Business Profile secondary categories, services, photos, posts and review replies, which public data could not show.

8. Consider booking, chat and local pages

After the quick wins, evaluate live scheduling or chat, test page speed and Core Web Vitals, and plan dedicated pages for communities such as Aurora and West Harrison if there is local search evidence.

Roadmap

Days 1 to 10: Consistency and links

Select the city name and update every profile, blog title and title tag. Fix the two broken implant page links, then write the implant and Savings Program meta descriptions.

Days 11 to 20: Proof and video

Build the before-and-after gallery with consented cases, link it from the cosmetic pages, create the YouTube channel and upload the existing Vimeo videos with links back to the site.

Days 21 to 30: Freshness and validation

Add article dates and image alt text, then check Search Console and analytics for the changes. Begin monitoring local search and AI mentions, and request owner access for profile checks.

Expected Outcomes

The audit frames these as objectives to work toward and measure, not as guaranteed results.

One consistent local identity

The same city name appears on the website, Google, social profiles and directories, addressing the audit's biggest local issue.

Cleaner key pages

Working links and benefit-driven meta descriptions on the implant and savings pages.

Visible proof for cosmetic care

A gallery that matches the smile-transformation message the site already promotes.

Better readiness for AI search

Clearer entity signals and dated content so FAQ-rich pages are easier for AI tools to interpret and cite.

Measured follow-through

Search Console, analytics and appointment requests used to confirm each change had an effect.

More From the Report

How the scoring works

Each area is scored out of 10 from a crawl of public pages and live Google data. Overall SEO averages 15 scorable website factors, and overall presence averages six categories. Anything that depends on the owner dashboard or paid tools is marked Unable to Verify and left out of the score.

AI search readiness

Question-based service content, such as pages on removing implants or whether someone is too old for them, and named practitioner credentials give AI tools good material. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overview visibility could not be confirmed, so I recommend monitoring instead of assuming presence.

Conversion path review

Trust signals and testimonials each score 8 of 10, supported by credentials, a practice history of more than 40 years, CareCredit financing and named patient testimonials. The gaps are real-time booking, chat and visual proof, which make the path from interest to appointment shorter.

What I Would Measure

  • Search Console impressions, clicks and click-through rate
  • Meta descriptions and broken links fixed
  • City name consistency across profiles
  • Gallery and YouTube launched
  • Appointment requests and calls
  • AI search mentions

Important Notes

Public data could not confirm Google Business Profile categories, services, photos, posts, Q&A or review replies, any AI mentions, backlinks, page speed or Core Web Vitals, so those items need owner access or tools. The scores are the audit's own assessment, not independently verified performance.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the biggest issue found for Oyler Family Dentistry?

The report ranks the city-name split first. The website, Google and Yelp use Greendale, while several social and directory profiles and one blog title use Lawrenceburg for the same address, giving NAP consistency a score of 4 of 10.

How does the practice score overall?

Overall SEO and local SEO score 7 of 10 each, AI search and overall digital presence score 6, and the opportunity score is 8. The report describes a strong clinical reputation not yet matched by execution.

Why do the Google reviews score so high while local SEO does not?

A 5.0 rating from 613 reviews earns 10 of 10 on rating and count. Local SEO is held back by the city-name split, citations that repeat it, and no dedicated pages for nearby communities.

Does the audit say the practice appears in AI answers?

No. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity inclusion could not be verified with public tools. The report only says the FAQ-style content gives good raw material, and recommends monitoring.

Is this an actual client engagement with results?

No. It is an independent portfolio audit of a publicly visible practice, dated August 26, 2026. It lists findings and a 30-day plan, and it does not claim results or promise rankings, traffic or new patients.
