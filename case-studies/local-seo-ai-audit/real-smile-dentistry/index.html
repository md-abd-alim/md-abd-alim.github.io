Real Smile Dentistry

Local SEO & AI Audit · Miami Beach, FL

Business Overview

Real Smile Dentistry is a multi-location dental practice headquartered at 757 Arthur Godfrey Rd in Miami Beach, Florida, with additional offices in North Miami and Hollywood. It has a 5.0-star Google rating from 320 reviews, four named dentists, a physiologic dentistry positioning and an Instagram account with 133K followers, so the foundation is unusually strong.

This independent portfolio study is an 8-page digital presence and AI search audit dated August 19, 2026. It scores the website, Google Business Profile, local SEO, social media, AI search and conversion, compares three nearby competitors, ranks the top 10 issues and ends with a 30-day quick-win list. Most of the upside sits in a handful of specific fixes rather than deep structural repair. Unverified items are marked as such rather than guessed. The plan is framed as recommendations, not guaranteed outcomes.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Current Situation

The audit finds a trusted, well-built practice whose AI visibility, profile detail and conversion tools have not caught up with its core strengths.

Strong site fundamentals

Indexing scores 9 of 10, HTTPS 10, technical SEO 8, content quality 9 and internal linking 9. The homepage has one clear H1, a deep service silo and three distinct location pages for Miami Beach, North Miami and Hollywood.

Weak spots on the page

Page speed scores only 4 of 10 because the homepage autoplays a background video on top of many hero images. Meta descriptions score 5, reading as a pipe-separated keyword list repeated across pages. Core Web Vitals could not be verified.

Profile services are thin

The Google Business Profile has a 9 for primary category, description and appointment link, but Services score 5 and Products 4. Both show a single outbound website link instead of itemized cards with names and images.

AI search is the biggest gap

Google AI Overview visibility is 1 of 10, with 0% of tracked positions from AI Overviews. For AI-related queries, third-party sites are cited instead of the clinic, even though the homepage already holds five structured FAQ pairs.

Competitors lead in places

North Miami Dental Center has fewer keywords (231 versus 405) but an AI Visibility score of 31 with confirmed ChatGPT and Gemini mentions. North Miami Dental Group ranks for 459 keywords. Real Smile clearly leads All Smiles General Dentistry.

Conversion tools are uneven

Click to call scores 10 of 10 across all three locations, and trust signals and testimonials score 9. But there is no live chat (0), no before-and-after gallery (3), and booking is a request form rather than confirmed instant scheduling (6).

What Happens If These Issues Are Not Fixed

If these gaps stay open, the practice's reputation and social reach convert less well than they could.

Invisible in AI answers

While third-party sites are cited for local dental questions, patients asking AI tools may be pointed to directories rather than the clinic, even though its content is already citation-ready.

Slow first impressions

A heavy autoplay hero video and many hero images raise page-weight risk. Mobile visitors on slower connections may leave before the strong content and call buttons appear.

Split local authority

A second live domain, realsmilenorthmiami.com, carries similar North Miami content. If it is not intentional or redirected, it could fragment name, address and domain authority between two sites.

Unused visual proof

The practice promotes FirstFit veneers and its Smile Preview AI tool, yet has no results gallery. Cosmetic prospects comparing offices may choose one that shows real outcomes.

Missed high-intent searches

"Dentist miami beach fl" (1,600 searches a month) sits at position 12. Staying on page two leaves the largest non-branded local opportunity with competitors.

Dormant social audience

A 133K-follower Instagram account is described as underleveraged. Without links and cross-promotion to location pages, that attention does little to support bookings or search signals.

Recommendations & Action Plan

I recommend starting with the fixes that reuse content the practice already owns, then tackling the heavier technical and conversion items.

1. Investigate the second domain

Confirm with the practice whether realsmilenorthmiami.com is intentional. If it is a legacy site, 301-redirect it into the main domain to consolidate local authority, and keep the North Miami location page and profile pointing to one consistent URL.

2. Mark up the existing FAQs

Add FAQPage schema to the five homepage question-and-answer pairs on physiologic dentistry and airway health. The content is already written, so this is the fastest way to make it readable for AI Overviews and other answer engines.

3. Rewrite meta descriptions

Replace the repeated keyword list on the homepage and each location page with natural, benefit-led sentences. Distinct descriptions per page improve click appeal and remove the templated look flagged in the audit.

4. Target the main local keyword

Optimize the Miami Beach page specifically for "dentist miami beach fl", which has 1,600 monthly searches and ranks 12. Adjust headings, internal links and local detail rather than adding new thin pages.

5. Fill the profile's service cards

Replace the single outbound link in Services and Products with individually named, imaged cards for the practice's main treatments. This adds search-result real estate and makes the profile match the depth of the website.

6. Build a consented results gallery

Create a before-and-after page using FirstFit veneers and Smile Preview examples, with patient permission and clear context. Link it from cosmetic service pages and the profile to give visual proof at the decision point.

7. Add chat and clarify booking

Add a live chat tool such as Podium or Weave to capture visitors browsing the 20-plus service pages, and confirm whether true instant online booking is possible instead of the current request-based flow.

8. Lighten the page and steady the blog

Review the autoplay hero video and image weight with PageSpeed Insights, then space blog posts across weeks instead of batch-publishing. Cross-promote the North Miami Instagram account from the main 133K account.

Roadmap

Days 1-10: Clarify and mark up

Confirm the status of the second domain and redirect if appropriate, add FAQPage schema, rewrite meta descriptions and begin optimizing for the main Miami Beach keyword.

Days 11-20: Profile and proof

Populate Google Business Profile service and product cards, gather consented examples for the gallery, and publish the first version of the before-and-after page.

Days 21-30: Convert and pace

Launch live chat, confirm the booking approach, test page speed, schedule blog posts across a steady cadence and cross-link the Instagram accounts.

Expected Outcomes

These are the planning objectives the audit points toward, not promised results.

Measurable AI visibility

Move beyond 0% AI Overview presence and begin appearing among cited sources for relevant queries, tracked against the current baseline.

Stronger Miami Beach ranking

Improve on position 12 for "dentist miami beach fl" and widen organic keyword coverage from the current 405.

A fuller profile

Present itemized services and products on the Google Business Profile in place of a single outbound link.

Faster, clearer experience

Address the page-speed concern and give visitors a chat option and a clearer booking path.

Consolidated local signals

Resolve whether the second North Miami domain should be redirected so authority sits in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Real Smile score only 3 out of 10 for AI search?

Live tracking shows 0% of its tracked positions come from Google AI Overviews, and its own domain is not among the top cited sources for AI-related queries. Third-party sites are cited instead, despite strong FAQ content on the homepage.

What is the issue with realsmilenorthmiami.com?

A second live domain surfaced in search carrying similar North Miami content. If it is not intentional or properly redirected, it could split local authority. I recommend confirming its purpose with the practice and redirecting it if it is a legacy site.

Which quick win does the audit prioritize for conversion?

Three: a consented before-and-after gallery, a live chat widget and clearer instant booking. The practice already promotes FirstFit veneers and Smile Preview, so visual proof is missing, while click to call already scores 10 of 10.

How does Real Smile compare with its competitors?

It has 405 organic keywords, ahead of All Smiles General Dentistry (189) and North Miami Dental Center (231), but behind North Miami Dental Group (459). North Miami Dental Center shows stronger AI visibility, with a score of 31.

Is this a client engagement with measured results?

No. This is an independent portfolio study of a publicly visible practice. The 30-day plan is a set of recommendations, and results would depend on implementation, competition and data only the practice can access.
