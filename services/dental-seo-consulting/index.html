Why Dental Practices Need an SEO Strategy

Many practices face several problems at once: a profile needing work, treatment pages that miss search intent, or competitors stronger in local searches. Fixing everything at once isn’t practical, so a strategy helps with:

  • Unclear keyword targeting and content priorities
  • Limited visibility for relevant local searches
  • Pages that don’t answer patient questions
  • Technical issues needing investigation
  • Inconsistent measurement of SEO performance

The objective is to replace guesswork with an organized roadmap tailored to your practice.

My SEO Strategy & Consulting Services

Each service is scoped to your goals and the information available, and can be combined into a single roadmap or taken as separate pieces.

1. SEO Opportunity Assessment

I review your services, locations, website and current search presence.

  • Keyword opportunities
  • Website structure and profile information
  • Local competition and performance data

Findings establish a baseline.

2. Keyword Research & Intent Mapping

Research shows how patients search for your services.

  • Treatment and location terms
  • Patient questions
  • Pages mapped to topics

The aim is to connect intent, content and priorities.

3. Competitor & Market Analysis

I review competing practices’ public content and profiles.

  • Treatment-page coverage
  • Google Business Profile information
  • Local search positioning

Recommendations avoid assumptions about private competitor data.

4. Website & Profile Priorities

Your website and profile serve different but connected purposes.

  • Treatment-page relevance
  • Internal linking and structure
  • Information consistency
  • Technical recommendations

Each item is prioritized by relevance and importance.

5. SEO Roadmap & Action Plan

Research is most useful when it leads to clear next steps.

  • High-priority website and technical issues
  • Content and profile priorities
  • Suggested metrics and review intervals

You learn what to address first and what can follow.

6. Measurement & Strategic Review

Strategy should evolve as evidence becomes available.

  • Search Console and website performance
  • Organic queries
  • Tracked contacts and appointments

Rankings and traffic are indicators, not the whole picture.

How My Consulting Process Works

  1. Discovery and Goal Setting

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a dental SEO consultant do?

A consultant evaluates search visibility opportunities and recommends improvements to website content, local search presence, technical SEO and measurement.

Is SEO consulting different from implementation?

Yes. Consulting focuses on research, strategy and recommendations, while implementation carries out approved changes. The agreed scope determines what is included.

How often should a strategy be reviewed?

It depends on your needs, progress, competition and available data. Regular reviews keep priorities relevant.

Can SEO consulting guarantee more patients?

No. SEO can support discoverability and the online experience, but inquiries also depend on demand, competition and reputation.

Do you work with practices that already have an SEO provider?

Yes. A strategic review can give you an independent view of priorities and help you evaluate or coordinate existing work.
