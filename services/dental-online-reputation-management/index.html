Why Online Reputation Matters

Choosing a dentist is personal. Patients weigh location, services, reviews and overall online presentation. An unmanaged reputation can create avoidable problems:

  • Inconsistent review activity across platforms
  • Feedback with little or no response
  • Recurring concerns that are hard to spot
  • Incomplete or outdated practice information
  • No consistent process for requesting genuine feedback

Reviews must be managed ethically. No strategy can guarantee a particular rating, a number of reviews or the removal of legitimate negative feedback.

My Online Reputation Management Services

Each service is scoped to your platforms, patient communication processes and the access available, with privacy and platform policies in mind.

1. Review Audit & Assessment

I assess your public review presence to find patterns and gaps.

  • Ratings and review recency
  • Feedback themes
  • Response practices
  • Information consistency

The audit sets a starting point for your strategy.

2. Google Review Strategy

A practical process that encourages authentic feedback without manipulating the outcome.

  • Right moments to invite feedback
  • A simple review process
  • Clear, professional request messaging
  • Platform policy and privacy compliance

No incentives or pressure to leave positive reviews.

3. Professional Review Responses

Responding shows that your practice takes feedback seriously.

  • Guidelines for positive and negative reviews
  • Spotting feedback needing internal attention
  • Avoiding defensive language

Responses must never confirm that someone was a patient.

4. Monitoring & Feedback Analysis

I organize public feedback into useful themes.

  • Communication and scheduling
  • Staff interactions
  • General service experience

Online feedback doesn’t represent every patient.

5. Competitor Reputation Analysis

I review how nearby practices appear and respond publicly.

  • Public ratings and review patterns
  • Review recency
  • Response approaches

The goal is useful insight, not simply a higher rating.

6. Website Trust Signals

Your website should present accurate, credible information.

  • Professional and business details
  • Authentic testimonials
  • Links to legitimate review platforms
  • Consistency with your Google Business Profile

Testimonials require genuine consent and privacy compliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you remove negative Google reviews?

No one can guarantee the removal of legitimate negative reviews. Reviews that violate platform policies may be reported, but the platform decides whether to remove them.

Can reputation management improve patient trust?

A consistent process for requesting genuine feedback, responding professionally and presenting accurate information can help prospective patients evaluate a practice more confidently.

Do you recommend buying Google reviews?

No. Reviews should reflect genuine experiences and follow platform policies. Fake or manipulative reviews can damage trust.

How long does reputation management take?

It depends on review activity, current processes and implementation. It is generally an ongoing activity, not a one-time fix.

What do you need from me to get started?

Links to your public profiles and website, plus a brief description of how your team currently requests and answers reviews.
