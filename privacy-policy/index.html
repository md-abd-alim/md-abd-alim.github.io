Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Md. Abdul Alim (“I”, “me”) handles personal information when you visit md-abd-alim.github.io or contact me about dental SEO and AI search optimization services. I keep data collection to a minimum.

Information I Collect

This website does not have accounts, comment sections or contact forms. I use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the website, including which pages are viewed and how visitors interact with the site. GA4 may collect technical and usage information such as device and browser details, approximate location, pages visited, and online identifiers. I also receive personal information when you choose to contact me, for example:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or WhatsApp details
  • Your practice name, website and location
  • Any details you share about your goals or current marketing
  • Booking details you enter on Calendly, such as name, email and chosen time

Like any website, the hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.

How I Use Your Information

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To schedule and hold consultations
  • To prepare proposals, audits or recommendations you have asked for
  • To keep records of our communication

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on services operated by others, which may receive technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads their content:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Font Awesome) deliver fonts and icons.
  • YouTube provides the intro video. It loads in privacy-enhanced mode and only after you click play.
  • Calendly, WhatsApp, email and phone providers process your messages and bookings if you use them.

These services have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for their practices.

Cookies

Google Analytics 4 may use first-party cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visitors and understand website usage. Other embedded third-party services, such as YouTube or Calendly, may also use cookies when you interact with them. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google’s available controls, including the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, where applicable.

Sharing Your Information

I share personal information only with service providers needed to respond to you, such as email, messaging or scheduling tools, or when required by law. I do not sell or rent your information.

Data Retention and Security

I keep communication records only as long as needed to respond to you and manage any working relationship, or as required by law. I use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
