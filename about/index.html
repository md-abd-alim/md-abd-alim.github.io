About Md. Abdul Alim

Independent dental SEO and AI search optimization consultant for U.S. dental practices.

I’m Md. Abdul Alim, an independent dental SEO and AI search optimization consultant. I work with U.S. dental practices that want to be easier to find in Google Maps, local search and AI-powered search, and easier to contact once patients reach their website.

I have 2+ years of experience in Dental SEO and have developed 21 dental SEO strategies as independent portfolio work, including 6 in-depth case studies for U.S. dental practices. This is where I refine my method: researching how patients search, reviewing Google Business Profiles and websites, studying local competitors and turning the findings into a prioritized plan.

My approach is simple: understand how patients search, find what is holding a practice back and recommend the next best step. I’d rather explain a clear plan than make a big promise.

How I Work

Four principles shape every recommendation.

Patient-first thinking

I start with how real patients search, compare practices and decide to call, then work back to your profile and website.

Evidence over promises

Recommendations rest on data and research. I don’t guarantee rankings, traffic or patient numbers.

Clear priorities

You get a ranked list of what to do first and why, not a long list of unconnected tasks.

Plain-language reporting

You see what was done, what was measured and what it suggests, in terms your team can act on.
